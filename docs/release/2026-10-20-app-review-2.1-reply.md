**2. Purpose and target audience**
Samnuan is practice-management software for law firms in Thailand. Its users are lawyers,
legal assistants, office managers and firm owners working inside a firm. It solves a daily
problem for Thai litigation lawyers: court hearing dates, statutory filing deadlines (computed
from the Thai Civil Procedure Code), case files, tasks and expenses are usually spread across
paper diaries, LINE chats and spreadsheets. Samnuan puts them in one place, warns the team
before deadlines, lets lawyers check their cases and log work from the courthouse on their
phone, and shows owners each lawyer's workload. The app is a work tool for legal
professionals; it does not provide legal advice to the public.

**3. How to set up and access the app**
Demo accounts (one firm, pre-filled with sample cases, hearings and tasks):
- Firm Owner (full access, incl. reports, finance, team management): `appreview-owner@samnuan.com` / `<password>`
- Lawyer (standard staff account): `appreview-lawyer@samnuan.com` / `<password>`

Steps: open the app → enter email and password → เข้าสู่ระบบ (Log in). The UI is in Thai.
Main features are on the bottom tabs: Home (today's hearings, deadlines, workload), Calendar,
Cases, Tasks, Team, and "อื่น ๆ" (More: expenses, clients, reports, knowledge base, settings).
Anyone can also create a new firm in the app via "สมัคร" (Sign up) with a free 30-day trial;
staff join a firm through an email invitation from the firm owner.
Account deletion: More (อื่น ๆ) → My account (บัญชีของฉัน) → Delete account and personal data
(ลบบัญชีและข้อมูลส่วนตัว) → enter password → submit. The user immediately receives a request
number; the account and personal data are deleted within 30 days (the firm's case records,
which law firms are legally required to retain, are handled separately from personal account
data). Deletion can also be requested at https://samnuan.com/delete-account.
No sample files are required.

User-generated content: comments, tasks and documents are visible only to members of the same
firm (a private workspace, like a company workspace). There is no public feed, profile
discovery, or contact with users outside the firm. The firm owner can remove members at any time.

Payments: firms subscribe directly with us on our website (B2B, Guideline 3.1.3(c)). The app
does not sell anything, show prices, or link to any purchase flow. If a firm's plan lapses,
the app remains usable in read-only mode.

**4. External services used**
- Amazon Web Services – hosting, database and document file storage (S3)
- Expo Push Service with Apple Push Notification service / Firebase Cloud Messaging – push notifications
- Resend – transactional email (invitations, password reset, deadline reminders)
- iApp Technology (Thailand) Thai legal data API – search of Thai statutes and court decisions
- Thai Supreme Court public judgment search (deka.supremecourt.or.th) – links to public decisions
- Microsoft Graph (Outlook) – optional, firm-authorized email integration for case intake
- Google Maps API – travel distance/time to court for scheduling and mileage expenses
- Stripe – subscription billing on our website only (not used inside the app)
Authentication is our own email + password (with optional two-factor code and Face ID unlock).
The iOS app does not include any AI features and does not send user data to any AI service.
(Our separate web application offers optional AI tools; they are not part of this app.)

**5. Regional differences**
The app functions the same in all regions; there are no region-specific features or content.
It is designed for the Thai legal system (Thai-language UI, Thai court procedure and deadline
rules), so it is distributed in Thailand.

**6. Regulated industry / third-party material**
Samnuan is a productivity tool used by licensed lawyers and law-firm staff; it does not itself
provide legal services or legal advice, so no legal-services license is required to offer it.
Each firm is responsible for its own members' professional licensing. Thai statutes and court
decisions shown in the app are public-domain government material, accessed through the public
Supreme Court website and through a commercial licensed API from iApp Technology Co., Ltd.
Personal data is processed under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA); our privacy
policy is at https://samnuan.com/privacy.

Please let us know if you need anything else.
